**BLodgic Privacy Policy CCPA/CPRA Compliance Gap Analysis Report**

**1. Executive Summary of Compliance Status**

The BLodgic Privacy Policy demonstrates a basic framework for handling user data but lacks several critical elements required under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). Key areas of non-compliance include inadequate disclosures regarding consumer rights, insufficient details on data collection and sharing practices, and missing procedures for handling consumer requests. Immediate attention is required to address these gaps to ensure compliance with CCPA/CPRA.

**3. Recommendations for Each Gap**

- **Right to Know Disclosure:** Update the policy to include a detailed list of categories of personal information collected and the purposes for each category.
- **Right to Delete Disclosure:** Add a section outlining the consumer's right to request deletion of their personal information.
- **Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing:** Include information on how consumers can opt-out of the sale or sharing of their personal information.
- **Right to Correct Inaccurate Information:** Add procedures for consumers to request corrections to their personal information.
- **Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information:** Specify how sensitive personal information is handled and provide opt-out options.
- **"Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" Link:** Implement and display a conspicuous link on the homepage for opting out of sales/sharing.
- **Data Retention Policies:** Clearly state the data retention periods for different categories of personal information.
- **Authorized Agent Procedures:** Provide detailed procedures for handling requests through authorized agents.
- **Non-Discrimination Policy:** Include a statement ensuring non-discrimination against consumers exercising their rights.
- **Methods for Submitting Requests:** Offer at least two methods for submitting requests, such as a toll-free number and an online form.
- **Privacy Policy Accessibility:** Ensure the privacy policy is accessible and linked conspicuously on the website.
- **Service Provider Contracts:** Ensure contracts with service providers include restrictions on the use of personal information.
- **Training Requirements:** Implement training programs for employees on CCPA compliance and consumer rights.
- **Annual Metrics Reporting:** Begin compiling and reporting metrics on consumer requests in the privacy policy.
