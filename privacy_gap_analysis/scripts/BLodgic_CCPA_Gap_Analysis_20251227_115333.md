**1. Executive Summary of Compliance Status**

The BLodgic Privacy Policy provides a general overview of how personal information is managed, but it lacks several critical disclosures and mechanisms required under the CCPA/CPRA. This report identifies these gaps and provides recommendations to enhance compliance with California privacy laws.

**Gap 1: Right to Know Disclosure**
- **Missing:** Disclosure of categories and specific pieces of personal information collected, sources, business purposes, and third-party sharing.

**Gap 2: Right to Delete Disclosure**
- **Missing:** Information on consumers' right to request deletion of personal information.
- **Reference:** CCPA Section 1798.105

**Gap 3: Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing**
- **Missing:** Notice of the right to opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information and a 'Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information' link.
- **Reference:** CCPA Section 1798.120

**Gap 4: Right to Correct Inaccurate Information**
- **Missing:** Disclosure of consumers' right to correct inaccurate personal information.
- **Reference:** CPRA Section 1798.106

**Gap 5: Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information**
- **Missing:** Information on the right to limit the use and disclosure of sensitive personal information.
- **Reference:** CPRA Section 1798.121

**Gap 6: Notice at Collection**
- **Missing:** Notice at or before the point of collection about categories of personal information collected and purposes.
- **Reference:** CCPA Section 1798.100(b)

**Gap 7: Privacy Policy - Collection Disclosure**
- **Missing:** Detailed categories of personal information collected and purposes for use.
- **Reference:** CCPA Section 1798.130(a)(5)

**Gap 8: Third-Party Disclosure Requirements**
- **Missing:** Disclosure of categories of personal information disclosed for business purposes and third parties involved.
- **Reference:** CCPA Section 1798.130(a)(5)(D)

**Gap 9: Privacy Policy - Sharing and Selling**
- **Missing:** Disclosure of whether personal information is sold or shared.
- **Reference:** CCPA Section 1798.130(a)(5)(C)

**Gap 10: Methods for Submitting Requests**

- **Reference:** CCPA Section 1798.130(a)(1)

**Gap 11: Children's Data Protections**
- **Missing:** Affirmative authorization requirements for selling/sharing data of consumers under 13 and between 13-16 years.

**Gap 12: Non-Discrimination Policy**
- **Missing:** Statement on non-discrimination against consumers exercising their rights.
- **Reference:** CCPA Section 1798.125(a)

**Gap 13: Privacy Policy Accessibility**
- **Missing:** Assurance of accessibility and clarity of the privacy policy.

**Gap 15: Record Keeping Requirements**
- **Missing:** Maintenance of records of consumer requests and responses.

**Gap 16: Annual Metrics Reporting**
- **Missing:** Compilation and availability of metrics regarding consumer requests.
- **Reference:** CCPA Regulations Section 999.317(g)

**3. Recommendations for Each Gap**

**Gap 1-4, 6-9, 11-12:** Update the privacy policy to explicitly include disclosures on consumer rights, including the right to know, delete, opt-out, correct, and limit use of sensitive information. Ensure these rights are clearly articulated with instructions on how consumers can exercise them.

**Gap 5:** Add a section on the right to limit the use of sensitive personal information, specifying the categories considered sensitive and how consumers can exercise this right.

**Gap 10:** Provide at least two methods for submitting consumer requests, including a toll-free number and an online form.

**Gap 13:** Ensure the privacy policy is accessible and easy to read, with a clear and conspicuous link on the homepage.

**Gap 14:** Include language in contracts with service providers that mandates compliance with CCPA obligations.

**Gap 15-16:** Implement procedures for maintaining records of consumer requests and compile annual metrics on these requests, making them available in the privacy policy or on the website.

**4. Priority Level for Each Gap**

- **High:** Gaps 4, 5, 8, 13, 14
