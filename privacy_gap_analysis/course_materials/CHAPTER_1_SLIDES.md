**Privacy Gap Analysis = HIGH VALUE FOR AI:**
- ✅ Repetitive: Same CCPA sections every analysis
- ✅ Pattern matching: Finding disclosures in policy text
- ✅ Cross-referencing: Policy vs. 35 CCPA requirements
- ✅ Data extraction: PDF → text → chunks
- ✅ Report generation: Structured markdown + Word output

**What AI DOESN'T Do (Human Judgment Required):**
- ❌ Decide which gaps to fix first (business priority)
- ❌ Draft final legal policy language (attorney expertise)
- ❌ Assess business impact (strategic decision)
- ❌ Client communication (relationship management)
- ❌ Determine risk appetite (governance decision)

**The Sweet Spot:**
AI does the tedious 90%, humans make the critical 10% of decisions.
